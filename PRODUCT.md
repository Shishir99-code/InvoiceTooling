# Product

## Register

product

## Users

A solo tutoring business owner (one person, one password) who manages student relationships, logs tutoring sessions, and needs to generate invoices to send to parents. She's working in a familiar environment (her device, her email), moving as quickly as possible from "I tutored these sessions" to "an invoice is in the parent's inbox asking for Zelle payment."

**Context**: She knows her students and their schedules; she's not managing a large team. She wants clarity on who owes what at a glance, and a friction-free path to invoice generation and sending.

## Product Purpose

Replace Excel-based time tracking and invoicing with a single web app that lives on her device (or any device with a browser). Let her:
- Track students and their session history
- Log individual tutoring sessions with dates, durations, and amounts
- See unbilled hours and amounts by student at a glance
- Generate text-only invoices pre-populated with student details, session summary, and her Zelle handle
- Send invoices via her own email (no app-managed email service)
- Archive students and manage settings (Zelle info, invoice templates)

**Success**: From "I taught these sessions" to "invoice is ready to send" in 2-3 clicks, no spreadsheet.

## Brand Personality

**Minimal, elegant, refined.** A tool that doesn't get in the way. High craft, intentional design, expert confidence. No startup posturing, no corporate stiffness, no generic UI-kit defaults. Feels personal but professional.

## Anti-references

- **Startup-trendy**: No heavy gradients, glassmorphism, decorative animations, hero sections, or SaaS clichés.
- **Overly corporate/formal**: Not banking-app dark and stiff; nothing that feels intimidating or sterile.
- **Flat/generic**: Not the default shadcn color palette or off-the-shelf UI-kit look. Should feel considered and unique.

## Design Principles

1. **Craft over flash** — Every element is intentional. No decorative animations, no trendy effects, no noise. Respect the user's time.

2. **Clarity through hierarchy** — The tutor must see what matters at a glance: unbilled hours, who owes the most, what's ready to invoice. Strong visual order, not uniform cards.

3. **Subtle refinement** — One refined accent color adds personality and guides focus (links, highlights, accents). Refined typography and generous spacing elevate the feel without clutter.

4. **Expert confidence** — The interface assumes the user knows what she's doing. No over-explaining, no hand-holding, no tooltips that explain obvious things. Clear labels, predictable interactions.

5. **Timeless, not trendy** — Neutral palette (whites, blacks, grays) with one refined accent. OKLCH colorspace for precision. No version bloat; evergreen design that works in 5 years.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

**WCAG AA standard** (4.5:1 text contrast minimum, keyboard navigation, semantic HTML, ARIA where needed). The app is single-user and text-heavy (invoices, tables); high contrast and clear hierarchy serve both usability and accessibility.
