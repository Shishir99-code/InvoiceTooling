<objective>
Deliver the Settings vertical slice (SET-01, SET-02) end-to-end: the tutor sets and
edits her Zelle handle plus an editable email subject line and body template, saves
them, and the values persist. This is Wave 1 because invoice generation (Wave 2)
consumes the template + Zelle handle to render invoice text.

This plan also lays the **full schema foundation** for the whole phase in one place so
the schema push happens exactly once: it adds the `invoices` table, the `settings`
table, and the nullable `sessions.invoiceId` FK all at once, then pushes. Waves 2-4
build against the already-pushed schema with no further push.

Purpose: The template must exist before an invoice can be rendered from it; Settings is
where the tutor controls greeting/tone/closing and her Zelle handle (D-12/D-13).
Output: Live `settings`/`invoices` tables + `sessions.invoiceId`, a working `/settings`
page, and the top-nav gaining History + Settings destinations.
</objective>
