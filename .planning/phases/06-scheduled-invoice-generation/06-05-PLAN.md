<threat_model>
ASVS L1 — a background job behind an authenticated cron endpoint that writes money rows.
- **Auth (SC4):** the route reuses `isAuthorizedCronRequest` (constant-time `CRON_SECRET` bearer, fails
  closed when the secret is unset) — no new or weakened auth; an unauthenticated caller gets 401 and no
  generation runs. Verified by the unauth-401 acceptance criterion.
- **Idempotency / double-bill:** the monthly `lastInvoicedMonth` HWM guarantees at most one run per
  calendar month, and each per-student generation goes through the same atomic double-billing CTE — a
  re-invoked cron (Hobby may retry/skip) cannot double-bill or double-generate. Guard order verified.
- **Availability / blast radius:** per-student try/catch ensures one failing student never aborts the
  batch; a thrown error returns the generic 500 without leaking internals.
- **Timezone correctness:** the cadence day is resolved in `settings.timezone`, preventing an off-by-one
  fire around UTC midnight (Phase-4 precedent).
- **Never auto-sends:** the cadence only inserts `sent = false` invoices (via the helper) — no email is
  sent by the app (RINV-04 / PROJECT.md client-side-email-only).
- **Secret handling:** `CRON_SECRET` stays in env (Vercel), never in code. Deploy reminder:
  [[vercel-hobby-cron-constraints]], [[deploy-phase3-after-gap-fix]].
</threat_model>
